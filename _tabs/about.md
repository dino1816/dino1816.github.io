##  Hey, I'm FireBlu.

I'm a DevOps engineer with a homelab problem. What started as a single server in the corner of my room turned into a full rack of hardware — and I wouldn't have it any other way.

I spend most of my time breaking and rebuilding things in my homelab, automating whatever I can, and diving deep into containerisation and orchestration. When I'm not doing that, I'm probably spinning up a game server for me and my friends to play, have fun and get some extra ideas for the homelab.

##  The Homelab

I run a homelab at home with a mix of physical servers and VMs. It's where I test everything before it ever touches anything important. Game servers, self-hosted services, networking experiments — it all lives there.

No cloud. Just hardware, cables, and a UPS I probably should have bought sooner.

##  What This Site Is

I built this because I got tired of finding documentation that was either outdated, incomplete, or written for people who already knew the answer. Every guide here is something I've actually done myself — written while doing it, mistakes included.
